    Loans Central Dashboard

    Customers reply to marketing texts on four separate lender portals. Loans Central Dashboard collects every reply in one place. When a reply asks for a loan or for funds, it opens that customer's page and texts back asking for their full name and the last 4 of their SSN.

    1. Customer replies “I need a loan”
    2. The reply shows up on the central dashboard
    3. The customer's page opens by itself
    4. Today's notes are checked for earlier contact
    5. The verification text goes out after a 5-second countdown
